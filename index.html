Common questions

Does anything get uploaded? No. Recording, transcription, speaker labels and summaries all run on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I restore Pro on a new device? Settings, Support, Restore purchases, while signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel a subscription? In your device's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tacit Note Pro.

How long can a free recording be? Up to 3 minutes each, with no limit on how many. Pro removes the limit.

Where are the widgets? Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Tacit Note.

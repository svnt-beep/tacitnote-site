TacitNote Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Applies to TacitNote for iPhone and iPad.

TacitNote records speech and turns it into text. One person makes it. The whole point of the app is that your recordings and transcripts stay on your device. This page says exactly what the app does with data.

What stays on your device

  • Audio you record or import. Kept in the app's private storage. Never uploaded.
  • Transcripts, speaker names, summaries, action items, folders and edits. Kept in a database on your device. Never uploaded.
  • Settings, such as your language, word dictionary and appearance.

Transcription, speaker labels and summaries all run on your device. Summaries use Apple Intelligence on the device, not Apple's or anyone else's servers. TacitNote has no server that receives audio or text, uses no cloud speech service, and works in Airplane Mode.

When TacitNote uses the internet

  1. Downloading a different speech model, only if you tap Download in Settings. The file comes from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which sees your IP address and the file requested, as any website does. The standard model is already inside the app.
  2. Purchases. Handled by Apple's App Store, with RevenueCat managing subscriptions. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and a random identifier the app creates, so it can tell the app whether you have Pro and restore it. It never receives audio, text or your name. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is the complete list. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. If you share crash reports with developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash data. These reports never include your audio or text.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record, and only while you are recording.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you when a transcript is ready.

Sharing and export

When you export or share a transcript or audio file, it goes only to the app you pick in the share sheet. What happens after that is up to that app.

Deleting your data

Delete a recording and its audio, transcript, speaker names and summary are removed. Settings, Privacy, Delete all data removes every recording at once. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

TacitNote is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. TacitNote will never start uploading your audio or transcripts. That would be a different product.
